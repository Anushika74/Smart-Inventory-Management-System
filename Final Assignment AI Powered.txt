Final Assignment 
AI-Powered Inventory Management System 
Module: Java Web Application Development 
Assignment Title 
Smart Inventory Management System with AI-Based Business Insights 
Background 
Modern businesses use Artificial Intelligence (AI) to analyze inventory and sales data to make 
smart business decisions. Companies such as Amazon and Walmart use intelligent systems to 
predict stock shortages, identify sales trends, and improve inventory management. 
In this assignment, students are required to enhance their existing Java Web Inventory System 
by integrating AI/ML-based features. 
Assignment Objectives 
Students should be able to: 
• Develop a complete Java Web application 
• Manage inventory and sales records 
• Analyze data using AI/ML techniques 
• Generate intelligent business suggestions 
• Visualize business analytics using charts 
• Integrate Java systems with AI tools or APIs 
Scenario 
You are developing a Smart Inventory Management System for a supermarket or retail shop. 
The system should: 
• Manage products and stock 
• Record sales transactions 
• Analyze sales and inventory data 
• Predict future stock requirements 
• Generate intelligent recommendations for business owners 
Functional Requirements 
Part 1 – Core Inventory System 
Students must implement the following modules: 
1. User Authentication 
• Login 
• Logout 
• User roles (Admin/Staff) 
2. Product Management 
• Add products 
• Update products 
• Delete products 
• Search products 
3. Inventory Management 
• Add stock 
• Update stock quantity 
• View available stock 
• Low stock alerts 
4. Sales Management 
• Record sales 
• Generate bills/invoices 
• Store sales history 
5. Reports 
• Daily sales report 
• Monthly sales report 
• Product stock report 
Part 2 – AI/ML Enhancement 
Students must implement at least FOUR intelligent features. 
AI Feature 1 – Smart Restock Prediction 
The system should analyze: 
• Product sales 
• Remaining quantity 
• Historical demand 
Then provide intelligent suggestions such as: 
“Product A may run out within 5 days.” 
“Recommended restock quantity: 50 units.” 
AI Feature 2 – Fast & Slow Moving Product Analysis 
The system should identify: 
• Fast-selling products 
• Slow-selling products 
• Dead stock 
The analysis should be displayed using charts. 
AI Feature 3 – Sales Trend Analysis 
Analyze: 
• Daily sales 
• Weekly sales 
• Monthly sales 
Then predict future trends. 
Example: 
“Sales increase during weekends.” 
AI Feature 4 – Intelligent Alerts 
Generate alerts for: 
• Low stock 
• Overstock 
• Sudden sales drops 
• Expiring products
